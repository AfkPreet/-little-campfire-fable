# Decisions

Every decision as chosen / rejected / why. Newest sections at the bottom. Pick this up weeks later and you should know why things are the way they are.

## 1. Locked in the pre-build interview (spec section 2; do not revisit)

| Area | Chosen | Rejected | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Genre | Cozy camping sim | Survival with hunger and warmth meters | Threat kills the serene feel; the hook is curiosity instead |
| Pressure | One positive-only Contentment level, no fail state | Soft or real survival stakes | Satisfaction should only rise; nothing punishes |
| Look | 2D side-view storybook, layered parallax | 3D diorama, 2D top-down, pixel art | Most reliably gorgeous from pure code |
| Art source | 100% drawn in code | AI-generated sprites and backgrounds | Code art can bounce, blink and relight; generated art cannot, and its style drifts |
| Palette | Muted pastels | Bright candy pastels, warm picture-book | Serene first, cute second; relights beautifully |
| World | Claude invents the setting, hero and cast | A fixed setting | One coherent world, locked in `ART_BIBLE.md` |
| Food | Fishing and foraging | Hunting, traps, gardening | Eating cute critters breaks kawaii; gardening adds scope |
| Feel | Gentle tap-timing fishing; drag-and-stir recipe discovery | Skill minigames, idle auto-play | Engaging without stress |
| Shelter | Pitch a tent, then decorate | Build from parts, one-tap tent | A satisfying setup plus self-expression |
| Structure | 7-day trip on a soft clock, then optional free play | Endless sandbox, several campsites in v1 | A clear arc without 3x the art |
| Economy | Starting budget; sell extras to earn | Fixed budget only, daily allowance | Creates the core loop |
| Hooks | Journal and mystery at full depth; companion and rare moments light | All four at full depth | Polish over breadth; the journal is the spine |
| Sound | Lo-fi plus nature; music files from Higgsfield; SFX, ambience and fallback score in code | All-code music, no music | The game must sound complete without files |
| Platform | Browser, touch-first, landscape | Keyboard-first, phone portrait | Mostly played on iPad; mouse still works |
| Build | Autonomous milestones with screenshot self-review | Pausing for review, one straight pass | Self-critique is where visual quality comes from |
| Ship | Vercel | Local only, Higgsfield community | A shareable link that opens on iPad |

## 2. The world (M0)

Two full concepts were generated by independent designers under different regional angles (a Kodagu backwater; a northern-Vietnam karst lake) and compared against the spec's rules: serene, kawaii, side-view layering, relighting, specific-not-generic, respectful, cast originality, title. Three further angles (northern lakes, islands, wildcard) were cut when the usage limit stopped the panel; the two finished concepts were strong enough to decide between.

| Area | Chosen | Rejected | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Setting | Nerale Thota on Manjukere, a coffee-estate reservoir backwater in Kodagu (Western Ghats, Karnataka) | (a) Lantern Lake, a karst lake in northern Vietnam; (b) a Kashmiri lake with shikaras; (c) a Kerala backwater with houseboats | It is the player's own region (five hours from Bengaluru), so the whole game is personal. Stacked Ghats ridges with mist bands between them are exactly the Alto-style parallax the spec wants, and morning manju burning off is the game's misty-morning weather for free. Karst towers were the most distinctive silhouette but came with Vietnamese diacritics in UI fonts and a cultural check we could not run. Kashmir carries sensitivities; Kerala has no ridges to layer. |
| Hero | Chikki, an Indian palm squirrel (three-striped) with a "curious tail" that curls into a "?" in idle | (a) Malabar giant squirrel; (b) bonnet macaque; (c) an otter kit juggling pebbles (from the Vietnam concept) | Bengaluru's own critter: every balcony has one. Three cream stripes plus an oversized tail read at 90 px with no other detail, and the "?" tail makes the Curious pillar visible. The giant squirrel fights "small and round" and drifts toward red-panda silhouettes; monkeys read mischievous, not serene. The otter was lovely but belongs to its lake. |
| Shopkeeper | Doddamma, an old water buffalo in half-moon spectacles and a marigold shawl | (a) sloth bear; (b) Asian elephant | A grandmotherly buffalo is specific to the place and patient by nature. A bear is black (forbidden) and reads as Baloo; an elephant is the default "India" animal and awkward at a counter. |
| Companion | Puttu, an Indian spotted chevrotain (mouse deer) | (a) Malabar whistling thrush; (b) Malabar gliding frog | A spotted bean with huge eyes that peeks from thickets is kawaii and native. A bird cannot sleep by the fire; the frog's colors are neon-adjacent. Risk logged: must not look like Chikki at 60 px (spots, upright ears, no tail). |
| Guardian | Bangari, an ancient humpback mahseer lit like a paper lantern | (a) a river dolphin spirit; (b) a giant lotus koi | Mahseer is this river's own legend and its palm-sized scales explain the Glimmering Scale clue. The Gangetic dolphin has nothing to do with the Kaveri; a lotus koi is the generic East-Asian answer. |
| Title | Little Monsoon | (a) Coracle Cove; (b) Manjukere Days; (c) Lantern Lake | Cozy, two words, easy to say, keeps "Little" from the working title, and a web search found no game of that name. Coracle Cove collides with Coral Cove and Corsair Cove; Manjukere Days is opaque to non-Kannada speakers; Lantern Lake belongs to the rejected world. |
| Currency | Cowries (kavade); icon a cream shell with a rose lip; `c` in code | (a) rupees; (b) coffee beans | Real historical money that every Karnataka grandmother still throws in chowka bara, and shells beside a lake feel like treasure. Rupees break the storybook scale and invite price-checking; beans confuse with Kaapi Powder and read as pebbles at 24 px. |
| Lead instrument | Venu (bamboo flute), low and breathy | santoor, veena as lead | The flute is the sound of the Ghats and synthesizes convincingly as a breathy sine; the veena voices the fallback plucks instead. |
| Accents | laterite #C99886, reservoir #8FB5B0, marigold #DFBE8F, jamun #9A7F9F (all 20-36% HSB sat) | lotus pink, bamboo, indigo, slate from the Vietnam concept | These four carry the place: red earth, teal water, estate marigold, jamun purple. |

Grafted from the Vietnam concept: paper lanterns under the shop awning and the lantern motif for the Contentment HUD; the translucent-body idea for the Firefly Catfish; egret V-shapes in the day sky; the "foot-rowed far boat" became a far coracle fisher by day (cut first if time runs short).

## 3. Tech (M0)

| Area | Chosen | Rejected | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stack | Vite 8 + TypeScript 5.9 + Phaser 3.90.0 (pinned) | Phaser 4.x (released), PixiJS, raw Canvas, Three.js | Spec asks for latest 3.x pinned; Phaser 4 is a different API surface and the 3.x FX pipeline (bloom, vignette) is well documented. |
| Retina | Canvas backing = CSS size x min(DPR, 2), Scale.FIT, and every camera zooms by `canvasH / 720` so scene code works in logical units | Scale.RESIZE (blurry on Retina), scaling all coordinates by DPR by hand | One `applyViewport()` call per scene; text is sharp via `setResolution(DPR)`. |
| Art pipeline | Paint each sprite once into a Canvas 2D texture at 2x, display at 0.5 scale via `img()` | Phaser Graphics every frame, SVG rasterizing | Gradients, soft shadows and glow are easy in Canvas 2D; textures are cheap to draw. Phaser's Graphics gradient fill shows triangle seams on rounded shapes, so gradients only ever come from painted textures. |
| Relighting | Far bands painted near-white and tinted with the actual light color; near layers painted in real colors and multiplied by a tint | Repainting every band per lighting change | One `setTint` per band per frame, no repaints; atmospheric perspective falls out of the tint math in `lighting.ts`. |
| Fonts | @fontsource Baloo 2 + Patrick Hand, self-hosted, awaited at boot | Google Fonts CDN, Fredoka, Caveat | Offline-safe and iPad-safe; Baloo 2 is rounder than Nunito. |
| Screenshots | `npm run shots <milestone>` starts Vite in-process and drives the pre-installed Playwright Chromium | A separate dev-server step | One cross-platform Node script; no bash. |
| Audio | Web Audio API directly, no Phaser sound | Phaser sound manager | Synthesis and crossfading need raw nodes; Phaser's manager adds little here. |

## 4. Process (M0)

| Area | Chosen | Rejected | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agent fan-out | Two or three focused implementers per workflow, verified inline by the integrator | Ten-agent panels at high effort | The first three parallel workflows (11 + 3 + 4 agents) exhausted the account's 5-hour usage limit in twenty minutes and died mid-file. Smaller fan-outs keep the build moving. |
| Salvage | Keep partial modules from the dead agents and finish them | Rewrite from scratch | `ui/kit.ts`, `fx/particles.ts`, `fx/textures.ts`, `audio/engine.ts` and most of `data/*` were complete and typechecked after small fixes. |

## 5. M0 outcomes

| Area | Chosen | Rejected | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Horizon | Far shoreline at y 420 with the estate as a real middle ground (y 290-420); water y 420-600 | The bible's first draft (shoreline at 345, water 345-600) | The tall empty water band flattened the scene and the estate was a strip of squiggles; the lower shoreline gives Kodagu's coffee rows and silver oaks room to read, and the mirrored reflection fills the water. |
| Ridge tints | `lighting.ts` exposes four white-band colors: far, ridgeMid, mid, estate | Two colors (far, mid) with the estate sharing mid | Three visibly stacked chains plus a greener estate are what make the depth read at every key. |
| Bands | Image strips per band, not TileSprites | TileSprites | A TileSprite allocates a world-sized canvas and re-uploads it on every tile-position change; strips are static quads. |
| Review | The integrator re-scores every artist board with stricter kawaii / want-to-play bars and issues one polish round with explicit art direction | Accepting the artists' self-scores | Self-scores were 0.5-1 point generous on the tail, the guardian body and the depth; one directed round fixed each. |
